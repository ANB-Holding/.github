# ANB Build Studio

Any idea from any ANB business becomes a working, testable product within days, on a platform the IT team designs, governs and ships.

**How to bring an idea:** open a ticket ("New idea" or "New AI agent") from Claude, Copilot or here on GitHub. The AI asks you a few questions, writes the spec and builds version 1 on a safe test copy. You try it, ask for changes, and validate. A senior developer reviews it before it goes live.

**Every ticket ends one way or the other:** Live, or Dropped with a reason.

Rules every AI and every person follows: see the `anb-brain` repository.
